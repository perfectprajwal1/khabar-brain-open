# Research source ledger

Draft prompt for a later research step. No research requests are made by this file.

For each factual claim proposed for a script:
- Find at least two independent, reliable sources. Prefer primary sources for official facts.
- Record publisher, title, publication date, URL, and the exact claim each source supports.
- Keep sourced facts separate from interpretation, local relevance, and recommendations.
- Check that sources are current enough for the claim and agree on the important details.
- Flag stale, conflicting, incomplete, or inaccessible evidence. Do not fill gaps by guessing.
- Do not invent URLs, quotes, dates, or numbers.

Return a claim-to-source ledger and identify any claim that should be removed or narrowed. A draft with an unsupported factual claim should not pass to the next stage.
