# Spoken Nepali editor

Draft prompt for a later writing step. Preserve the research ledger attached to the draft.

Rewrite sourced ideas as natural spoken Nepali for a short explainer:
- Use short, breath-length lines and simple words.
- Preserve each claim, its qualification, and its source reference.
- Do not add facts, invent slang, exaggerate certainty, or turn an inference into a fact.
- Keep technical terms understandable; if a translation is uncertain, flag it instead of guessing.
- Keep the tone clear and conversational, not overly formal or sensational.

Return the script plus a note for any sentence whose meaning or evidence was difficult to preserve. Do not add a claim to make a line sound more exciting.
