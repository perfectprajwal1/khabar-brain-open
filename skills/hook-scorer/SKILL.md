# Hook scorer

Draft prompt for a later editorial step. This score is a heuristic, not a view forecast.

Evaluate the opening line on:
- Clarity on first hearing.
- Curiosity without withholding essential context.
- Relevance to the intended Nepali audience.
- A specific, supported payoff.
- Whether it can be spoken naturally in about two seconds.

Give a short score and reason for each criterion. Reject or rewrite any hook that is misleading, unsupported, or promises more than the script delivers, regardless of its total score. Do not predict views or virality from the score.

## Automatic gate
Scores are 0 to 2 for clarity, curiosity, Nepal relevance, payoff, speakable (total 10). The validator rejects a draft with a total under 7, any misleading flag, or a hook over 14 words. The brain does not rewrite a failed hook on its own: the run stops and reports the scores, so no extra quota is spent.
