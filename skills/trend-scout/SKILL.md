# Trend scout

Draft prompt for a later research step. Do not treat this file as an active connection to a trend service.

Find ideas for useful digital-skill explainers for Nepali students and early-career workers. Do not select news, politics, or sports.

For every proposed topic:
- Record the platform/source, location or region, metric, and observation date.
- Separate observed interest from an inference about Nepal fit.
- If the source does not offer Nepal-specific data, label the signal as global or from the available region. Do not present it as a Nepal trend.
- Do not call a topic a top trend without dated, location-specific evidence.
- Prefer practical, safe demonstrations that can be shown clearly in a short video.

Return a short ranked list with the topic, evidence, locality caveat, confidence, and a possible hook. State when the evidence is too weak to rank. A ranking is an editorial choice, not a prediction of views or virality.
