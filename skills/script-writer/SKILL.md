# Script writer

Write the reel script from the research ledger only.

- 5 or 6 short lines, about 45 to 55 Nepali words, about 20 to 25 seconds. Devanagari only; English terms are written in Devanagari (च्याटजीपीटी, जिमेल) and added to the pronunciation list.
- Line 1 is the hook: a question or a surprising true fact, at most 12 words, says who it is for and what they get. No clickbait the script does not pay off.
- Middle lines: one idea per line, each cites claim ids. Facts keep their qualifiers ("can", "may", "usually").
- Last line: one concrete thing to do today. No "like, follow, subscribe".
- The brain chooses an emotion for every line by itself (see voice-director) and designs an arc, not one flat tone.
- Return flags for any line where the meaning was hard to keep. Never add a fact to make a line exciting.
